### 2. Brand Visibility Tracking
Once you've set up your brand profile — name, relevant categories, product description — rankscale.ai starts tracking how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers.
You get a **Visibility Score** that shows your overall presence across AI platforms. You can drill down to see which specific queries trigger brand mentions, which AI platforms mention you most often, and whether those mentions are positive, negative, or neutral.

This is where rankscale.ai really shines. You can add your competitors and compare your AI visibility side by side.

This makes it easy to identify gaps in your AI presence and prioritize the areas where your competitors are winning.
### 4. Sentiment Analysis
Not all brand mentions are created equal. Rankscale.ai analyzes the **sentiment** of AI-generated answers about your brand — positive, negative, or neutral.
This is surprisingly accurate for an automated tool. During my testing, rankscale.ai correctly identified a negative mention in a Perplexity answer that I would have missed on a quick scan. The tool flagged it, I investigated, and found an outdated article that needed updating.
