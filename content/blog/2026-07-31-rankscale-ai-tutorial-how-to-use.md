## Step 1: Run Your Initial Website Analysis
When you first log into rankscale.ai, the platform prompts you to run a **website analysis**. This is the foundation of everything else.
Here's what to do:
1. Enter your domain (e.g., `yourdomain.com`)
2. Choose a crawl depth — "Standard" is fine for most sites
3. Click "Analyze"
Rankscale will crawl your site and generate an **AI Search Readiness Score** (0-100%). This score measures how well your website is optimized for AI search engines across four dimensions:
- Content Quality & Relevance
- Authority & Trustworthiness
- Technical SEO & Page Structure
- User Engagement
**Pro tip:** Don't obsess over the initial score. Use it as a baseline. The real value comes from tracking how this score changes as you make improvements.

## Step 2: Set Up Your Brand Profile
After the website analysis, you need to create a **brand profile**. This tells rankscale.ai what to track.
You'll enter:
- **Brand name** — Your company or product name
- **Categories** — The industry or niche you operate in
- **Product description** — A brief, clear description of what you do
- **Key topics** — The subjects you want to be associated with
The more complete your brand profile, the more accurate your tracking will be. Take 10 minutes to get this right.
Once your brand profile is set up, rankscale.ai starts monitoring AI platforms for mentions of your brand. It takes a few hours to start seeing data, so don't worry if the dashboard is empty on day one.

## Step 3: Add Your Competitors
This is where things get interesting. Rankscale.ai lets you add competitors and compare your AI visibility against theirs.
To add competitors:
1. Go to the "Competitors" section
2. Enter a competitor's domain or brand name
3. Rankscale will automatically create their brand profile
4. Repeat for your top 3-5 competitors
Once competitors are added, you can use the **one-on-one comparison** view to see where you lead and where you're falling behind. The **matrix view** gives you a broader picture of the competitive landscape.
**Real example from my testing:** I added three competitors to my test brand's dashboard. Within a week, I discovered that one competitor was being mentioned in AI answers for a topic I hadn't even considered targeting. That insight alone was worth the setup time.

## Step 4: Review Your Visibility Dashboard
After a few days of data collection, your dashboard will start showing meaningful information. Here's what to look at first:
### Overall Visibility Score
This is your brand's total presence across all tracked AI platforms. A higher score means you're being mentioned more often and in more contexts.
### Platform Breakdown
See which AI platforms mention your brand most frequently. You might discover that you're strong in ChatGPT but invisible in Perplexity — which tells you where to focus your optimization efforts.
### Top Queries
These are the search queries that trigger AI mentions of your brand. They reveal what topics people are searching for when your brand comes up — and what topics you should create more content about.
### Sentiment Overview
This shows whether AI mentions of your brand are positive, negative, or neutral. A sudden drop in sentiment is worth investigating immediately.

## Step 5: Act on Your Insights
Data without action is just noise. Here's how to turn rankscale.ai's insights into real improvements.
### If Your AI Readiness Score Is Low
Focus on the weakest category. If "Technical SEO & Page Structure" is dragging your score down, run a technical audit. If "Content Quality & Relevance" is the issue, invest in better, more substantive content.
### If Competitors Outperform You on Specific Topics
Create content specifically targeting those topics. Use rankscale.ai's citation analysis to see what kind of content is driving your competitor's visibility, then create something better.
### If Sentiment Is Negative
Identify the source of the negative mentions. Is it an outdated article? A misattributed quote? A product issue? Fix the root cause, and rankscale.ai will show you when the sentiment improves.
### If You're Invisible on a Specific Platform
Different AI platforms have different preferences. ChatGPT might favor well-structured, authoritative content, while Perplexity might prioritize recent, news-style articles. Tailor your content strategy to the platforms where you want to be seen.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
### Mistake 1: Setting Up and Forgetting
Rankscale.ai is not a set-it-and-forget-it tool. Check your dashboard at least weekly, and act on the insights you find.
### Mistake 2: Tracking Too Many Competitors
Start with 3-5 competitors. More than that and the data becomes overwhelming without adding proportional value.
### Mistake 3: Ignoring Sentiment
Positive and negative mentions matter more than raw visibility. A brand that's mentioned often but negatively has a different problem than one that's invisible.
### Mistake 4: Not Using the Weekly Summaries
The weekly email digest is one of the best features. It saves you from logging in daily while keeping you informed of important changes.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues
Even with a well-designed tool, you might run into a few hiccups. Here's how to handle them.
### Problem: No Data After 24 Hours
**Solution:** Double-check your brand profile. Make sure the brand name, categories, and description are accurate. If everything looks correct, contact support — there may be a configuration issue on the backend.
### Problem: Competitor Data Seems Incomplete
**Solution:** Rankscale.ai builds competitor profiles automatically, but it works best when the competitor has a well-established web presence. For smaller competitors, you might need to manually refine the profile.
### Problem: Sentiment Analysis Seems Off
**Solution:** Sentiment analysis is never perfect. If you see a misclassified mention, flag it in the tool. This feedback helps improve the algorithm over time.
### Problem: The AI Readiness Score Doesn't Change
**Solution:** The score updates on a regular cadence, not in real time. If you've made significant changes to your site, give it a few days to reflect. Also, check that your changes align with the four categories the score measures.
